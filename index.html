How it works

From signup to your first confirmed payment in four steps — no paperwork, no bank-side merchant account, no waiting for API approval.

1

Create a free merchant account

Sign up with your business name and phone number. Your merchant account is free and takes minutes — that's the whole application.

2

Add your bKash number

Tell SarahPay the bKash number your customers should send money to — your regular personal or agent number works. Nagad, Rocket and more will be added the same way.

3

Get your API key — or just share your checkout link

Developers can plug in with an API key. No website code? Every merchant also gets a ready-made checkout link to share anywhere — a page, a post, a message.

4

Get paid — verified automatically

Your customer pays by bKash Send Money and enters the TrxID at checkout. Our SMS checker matches the TrxID, amount and sender, and marks the payment paid automatically.

How a payment confirms itself

Normally, someone has to open the bKash app, find the SMS and check every payment by hand. SarahPay does that checking for you.

Customer

Pays & enters the TrxID

The customer sends money to your bKash number with Send Money, then types the transaction ID from their confirmation SMS into your checkout.

→
SMS checker app

Reads the bKash SMS on your phone

A small Android app on your phone reads the incoming bKash SMS and securely forwards it to SarahPay the moment the money lands.

→
SarahPay

Matches three things, marks it paid

TrxID, amount and sender number must all match the pending order. When they do, the payment flips to paid — no manual checking, no screenshots to review.

Built for businesses that sell online in Bangladesh

Most small businesses here collect money on a personal bKash number and confirm orders from SMS screenshots. SarahPay keeps that habit — and removes the manual work around it.

No API key needed to start

Your checkout link works on its own — share it and start taking payments. The API key is there when you want a deeper integration, not a gate you have to pass first.

Auto-verified by bKash SMS

An Android app on your phone reads each incoming bKash SMS and forwards it to SarahPay. Payments verify themselves against the real SMS — not against a screenshot a customer could edit.

No merchant account required

Money goes straight to your own bKash number by Send Money. There's no merchant account to open and no settlement account to wait for — the money is already yours when it arrives.

Confirmed in moments, not hours

As soon as the SMS lands and the three checks match, your order shows as paid. You pack and ship with confidence instead of re-checking your balance between orders.

A TrxID can only be used once

Each transaction ID is tied to one order and one amount. The same TrxID can't be replayed against a second order, and a mismatched amount never confirms.

Questions

Do I need a bKash merchant account?

No. Customers pay your regular bKash number using Send Money, so there's no merchant account to apply for. The money lands in your own wallet, as it does today.

How does SarahPay know a payment really happened?

A small SMS checker app on your Android phone forwards each incoming bKash SMS to SarahPay. When a customer enters a TrxID at checkout, we match it against that SMS — TrxID, amount and sender number all have to agree before the payment is marked paid.

Do I need a website or an API key?

Neither. Every merchant gets a checkout link that works on its own — share it anywhere. If you do have a website or app, you can use your API key to create checkouts and receive a webhook when a payment is paid.

Which payment methods can I accept?

bKash works today. Nagad, Rocket, Upay, MCash, TAP, Pathao Pay and Cellfin are in progress and will join the same checkout as they launch.

When can I create my merchant account?

Self-serve signup opens soon. SarahPay is already processing real payments for its first merchant — a mosque donation gateway — while we finish the public signup.
